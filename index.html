Sri Lankan natural Rubber

From the island's soil to the world.

Discover Sri Lanka's natural rubber industry, its history, growing regions and the products created from one of the country's important plantation crops.

A SHORT STORY

1876

Rubber Arrives in Sri Lanka

Rubber seedlings were planted at Henarathgoda Botanical Gardens in Gampaha, marking an important beginning for rubber cultivation in the country.

Today

A value added industry

Sri Lanka produces natural rubber and manufactures products such as gloves, tyres, hoses, footwear and rubber sheets for local and international markets.
